# Kunwa Business Consulting Services Ltd

Official repository for **Kunwa Business Consulting Services Ltd**, a premier consultancy specializing in International Negotiations, Procurement Contracts, Procurement Outsourcing, and Career Development Coaching.

## 🏢 About the Company
Based in Douala, Cameroon, Kunwa Business Consulting Services Ltd drives operational excellence and strategic supply chain transformation across African markets and beyond. Led by Managing Consultant **Sophie W Tafon** and CIPS-trained experts, we provide rigorous global standards, risk mitigation, and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) optimization.

* **Professional Consultancy Services:** Comprehensive details on contract drafting, outsourcing, and supply chain management.
