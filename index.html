About Us

Based in Douala, Cameroon, Kunwa Business Consulting Services Ltd is a premier consultancy dedicated to driving operational excellence and strategic supply chain transformation across African markets and beyond.

Led by Managing Consultant Sophie W Tafon and certified experts trained through CIPS (The Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply), we combine deep industry knowledge with rigorous global standards. Our leadership brings extensive expertise in International Negotiations, Procurement Contracts, Procurement Outsourcing, and specialized sourcing frameworks including FF&E Procurement, Transportation Procurement, and E-procurement.

We are committed to fostering enduring trust, mitigating cross-border risk, and delivering measurable value through Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) optimization for businesses scaling in Africa.
